The 99 Names of Allāh

The ninety-nine Names of Allāh.
الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَى

The Prophet ﷺ said that Allāh has ninety-nine Names, and whoever enumerates them will enter Paradise. To enumerate them is to learn them, reflect on their meanings and live by them. Each Name opens a door to knowing Allāh: the one who knows Him as ar-Raḥmān hopes in Him, and the one who knows Him as al-Ḥasīb is mindful before Him. Calling on Him by His Names is commanded in the Qurʾān: “To Allāh belong the Most Beautiful Names, so call upon Him by them.” The Murid recites them to deepen maʿrifah and to make duʿāʾ through them.
