The Names of the Prophet ﷺ

The names of the Prophet ﷺ.
أَسْمَاءُ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ

The Prophet ﷺ said, “I have names: I am Muḥammad, I am Aḥmad, I am al-Māḥī through whom Allāh effaces disbelief, I am al-Ḥāshir at whose feet the people will be gathered, and I am al-ʿĀqib.” Each of his names describes a quality of his perfection, a station or a gift given to him. Learning them increases the Murid in knowledge and love of him ﷺ, and love of him is the foundation of the path.
