Sūratul Fātiḥah

The Mother of the Book; the gathering opens with it.
سُورَةُ الْفَاتِحَةِ

Sūratul Fātiḥah is Ummul Kitāb, the Mother of the Book, and the Prophet ﷺ called it the greatest Sūrah in the Qurʾān. It is recited in every rakʿah of every Ṣalāh, and the gathering of dhikr opens with it. In seven Āyāt it holds the whole of the Dīn: praise of Allāh, His Lordship and Mercy, worship of Him alone, and the plea for guidance on the Straight Path. Allāh answers the servant after each Āyah, so the Murid recites it with presence, as one in conversation with his Lord. It is a cure and a protection, and a key to the opening of every affair.

1

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيْمِ

A-ʿŪDHU BILLĀHI MINASH SHAYṬĀ-NIR RAJĪM

I seek refuge in Allāh from Shayṭān, the rejected one.

2

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيْمِ ۝١

BIS-MIL-LĀHIR RAḤMĀ-NIR RAḤĪM

In the Name of Allāh, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

3

ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ۝٢

ALḤAMDU LIL-LĀHI RABBIL-ʿĀLAMĪN

All praise is for Allāh, Lord of the Worlds.

4

ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ۝٣

AR-RAḤMĀ-NIR-RAḤĪM

Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

5

مَـٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ ۝٤

MĀLIKI YAWMID-DĪN

Master of the Day of Judgement.

6

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝٥

IY-YĀKA NAʿBUDU WA IY-YĀKA NASTAʿĪN

You [alone] we worship; and You [alone] we ask for help.

7

ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝٦

IH-DINAṢ-ṢIRĀṬAL MUSTAQĪM

Guide us to the Straight Path.

8

صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ ۝٧

ṢIRĀṬAL LADHĪNA ANʿAMTA ʿALAYHIM, GHAYRIL MAGH-ḌŪBI ʿALAYHIM WA LAD-ḌĀLLĪN

Not [the Path] of those who earn Your anger (wrath), nor of those who go astray.

9

آمِيْن

ĀMĪN
