Sūrah Yā Sīn

The heart of the Qurʾān.
سُورَةُ يٰسٓ

Sūrah Yā Sīn is described in a narration as the heart of the Qurʾān. It speaks of the Messengers and those who rejected them, the Signs of Allāh in the earth, the night, the sun and the moon, and the certainty of the Resurrection. Its opening Āyāt are also recited within the Ḥizbul Baḥr. The ʿUlamāʾ relate that it is recited for the ease of one’s affairs, for the sick and the dying, and for those who have passed on. Recited with reflection, it softens the heart and renews certainty in the meeting with Allāh.

1

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيْمِ

A-ʿŪDHU BILLĀHI MINASH SHAYṬĀ-NIR RAJĪM

I seek refuge in Allāh from Shayṭān, the rejected one.

2

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيْمِ

BIS-MIL-LĀHIR RAḤMĀ-NIR RAḤĪM

In the Name of Allāh, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

3

يسٓ ۝١

YAASEEN

Yā Sīn.

4

وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ ۝٢

WAL QUR’AANIL HAKEEM

By the Qur’an, full of Wisdom

5

إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ ۝٣

INNA KA LA MINAL-MURSALEEN

You are indeed one of the Messengers.

6

عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٍۢ مُّسْتَقِيمٍۢ ۝٤

ʿALAA SIRAATIM MUSTAQEEM

On a Straight Way.

7

تَنزِيلَ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ۝٥

TANZEELAL-ʿAZEEZIR RAHEEM

(It is a Revelation) Sent down by (Him), the Exalted in Might, Most Merciful.

8

لِتُنذِرَ قَوْمًۭا مَّآ أُنذِرَ ءَابَآؤُهُمْ فَهُمْ غَـٰفِلُونَ ۝٦

LITUN-DHIRAQAWMAMMAAUN-DHIRAAA-BAA-UHUMFA HUM GHAAFILUUN

In order that you may admonish (warn) a people, whose fathers had received no admonition and who therefore remain heedless (of the Signs of Allah).

9

لَقَدْ حَقَّ ٱلْقَوْلُ عَلَىٰٓ أَكْثَرِهِمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ ۝٧

LAQADHAQ-QALQAWLUʿALAAAK-THARIHIM FAHUMLAA YU’MINUUN

The Word is proved true against the greater part of them; for they do not believe.

10

إِنَّا جَعَلْنَا فِىٓ أَعْنَـٰقِهِمْ أَغْلَـٰلًۭا فَهِىَ إِلَى ٱلْأَذْقَانِ فَهُم مُّقْمَحُونَ ۝٨

IN-NAAJAʿALNAAFEEAʿA-NAAQIHIMAGH-LAALAN FA HIYA ILAL-ADH-QAANIFAHUMMUQMAHUUN

We have put yokes round their necks right up to their chins, so that they cannot bow their heads [their heads are forced up and they cannot see.]

11

وَجَعَلْنَا مِنۢ بَيْنِ أَيْدِيهِمْ سَدًّۭا وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ سَدًّۭا فَأَغْشَيْنَـٰهُمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُبْصِرُونَ ۝٩

WA JAʿALNAA MIM BAYNI AY-DEE HIM SAD-DAN(W) WA MIN KHALFI-HIM SAD-DAN(W) FA-AGH-SHAYNAA HUM FA-HUM LAA YUBSIRUUN

And We have put a bar in front of them and a bar behind them, and further, We have covered them up; so that they cannot see.

12

وَسَوَآءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَأَنذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنذِرْهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ ۝١٠

WA SAWAA-UN ʿALAY-HIM A-AN-DHARTA HUM AM LAM TUN-DHIR HUM LAA YU’MINUUN

The same it is to them: whether you admonish them or you do not admonish them: they will not believe.

13

إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلذِّكْرَ وَخَشِىَ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنَ بِٱلْغَيْبِ ۖ فَبَشِّرْهُ بِمَغْفِرَةٍۢ وَأَجْرٍۢ كَرِيمٍ ۝١١

INNA-MAA TUN-DHIRU MAN-IT-TABAʿADH-DHIKRA WA-KHASHIYAR-RAHMAANA BIL-GHAYBI FA-BASH-SHIR-HU BI MAGH-FIRATIN(W) WA-AJRIN KAREEM

You can only admonish such a one as follows the Message and fears the (Lord) Most Gracious, unseen: give such a one, therefore, good tidings of Forgiveness and a Reward most generous.

14

إِنَّا نَحْنُ نُحْىِ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ وَنَكْتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُوا۟ وَءَاثَـٰرَهُمْ ۚ وَكُلَّ شَىْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَـٰهُ فِىٓ إِمَامٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ ۝١٢

INNAA NAHNU NUHYIL MAWTAA WA NAKTUBU MAA QADDAMUU WA AA-THAARAHUM WA KULLA SHAY-IN AHSAY-NAAHU FEE IMAAMIM MUBEEN

Verily We shall give life to the dead, and We record that which they sent before, and that which they leave behind, and of all things have We taken account in a Clear Book (of evidence).

15

وَٱضْرِبْ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصْحَـٰبَ ٱلْقَرْيَةِ إِذْ جَآءَهَا ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ ۝١٣

WAD-RIB LAHUM MATHALAN AS-HAABAL QARYAH, IDH JAA’A-HAL-MURSALUUN

Set forth to them by way of a parable the (story of) the Companions of the City. Behold there came apostles to it.

16

إِذْ أَرْسَلْنَآ إِلَيْهِمُ ٱثْنَيْنِ فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَعَزَّزْنَا بِثَالِثٍۢ فَقَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُم مُّرْسَلُونَ ۝١٤

IDH ARSALNAA ILAY-HI-MUTH-NAYNI FA-KADH-DHABUU HUMAA FA-ʿAZ-ZAZNAA BI-THAALI-THIN FA-QAALUU INNAA ILAY-KUM MURSALUUN

When We (first) sent to them two apostles they rejected them: but We strengthened them with a third: they said “Truly we have been sent on a mission to you.”

17

قَالُوا۟ مَآ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا بَشَرٌۭ مِّثْلُنَا وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ مِن شَىْءٍ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا تَكْذِبُونَ ۝١٥

QAALUU MAA ANTUM ILLAA BASHARUM MITH-LUNAA WA MAA ANZALAR-RAHMAANU MIN SHAY-IN IN ANTUM ILLAA TAKDHIBUUN

The (people) said: “Ye are only men like ourselves; and (Allah) Most Gracious sends no sort of revelation: Ye do nothing but lie.”

18

قَالُوا۟ رَبُّنَا يَعْلَمُ إِنَّآ إِلَيْكُمْ لَمُرْسَلُونَ ۝١٦

QAALUU RAB-BU-NAA YAʿLAMU IN-NAA I-LAY KUM LA-MUR-SALUUN

They said: “Our Lord doth know that we have been sent on a mission to you:

19

وَمَا عَلَيْنَآ إِلَّا ٱلْبَلَـٰغُ ٱلْمُبِينُ ۝١٧

WA MAA ʿALAYNAA ILLAL-BALAAGHUL MUBEEN

“And Our duty is only to proclaim the clear Message.”

20

قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّا تَطَيَّرْنَا بِكُمْ ۖ لَئِن لَّمْ تَنتَهُوا۟ لَنَرْجُمَنَّكُمْ وَلَيَمَسَّنَّكُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌۭ ۝١٨

QAALUU INNAA TATAY-YARNAA BI-KUM LA IL-LAM TANTAHU LA NARJUMANNA-KUM WA LA YAMAS-SANNA-KUM MIN-NAA ʿA-DHAABUN ʿALEEM

The (people) said: “for us We augur an evil omen from you: if ye desist not we will certainly stone you and a grievous punishment indeed will be inflicted on you by us.”

21

قَالُوا۟ طَـٰٓئِرُكُم مَّعَكُمْ ۚ أَئِن ذُكِّرْتُم ۚ بَلْ أَنتُمْ قَوْمٌۭ مُّسْرِفُونَ ۝١٩

QAALUU TAA-IRUKUM MAʿA-KUM A-IN DHUK-KIRTUM BAL ANTUM QAW-MUM MUSRIFUUN

They said: “Your evil omens are with yourselves: (deem ye this an evil omen) if ye are admonished? Nay but ye are a people transgressing all bounds!”

22

وَجَآءَ مِنْ أَقْصَا ٱلْمَدِينَةِ رَجُلٌۭ يَسْعَىٰ قَالَ يَـٰقَوْمِ ٱتَّبِعُوا۟ ٱلْمُرْسَلِينَ ۝٢٠

WA JAAA-A MIN AQSAL MADEENATI RAJULUN YASʿAA QAALA YAA QAW-MIT-TABIʿUL MURSALEEN

Then there came running from the farthest part of the City a man saying “O my people! obey the apostles:

23

ٱتَّبِعُوا۟ مَن لَّا يَسْـَٔلُكُمْ أَجْرًۭا وَهُم مُّهْتَدُونَ ۝٢١

IT-TA-BIʿUU MAL-LAA YAS-A-LU-KUM AJ-RAN(W) WA HUM MUHTADUUN

“Obey those who ask no reward of you (for themselves) and who have themselves received Guidance.

24

وَمَا لِىَ لَآ أَعْبُدُ ٱلَّذِى فَطَرَنِى وَإِلَيْهِ تُرْجَعُونَ ۝٢٢

WA MAA LI-YA LAA AʿABU-DUL-LA-DHEE FATARA-NEE WA ILAYHI TURJAʿUUN

“It would not be reasonable in me if I did not serve Him Who created me and to Whom ye shall (all) be brought back.

25

ءَأَتَّخِذُ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةً إِن يُرِدْنِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ بِضُرٍّۢ لَّا تُغْنِ عَنِّى شَفَـٰعَتُهُمْ شَيْـًۭٔا وَلَا يُنقِذُونِ ۝٢٣

A-AT-TAKHI-DHU MIN DUUNI-HII AALIHATAN IN(Y)-YURID-NIR-RAHMAANU BI-DURRIL LAA TUGH-NI ʿAN-NI SHAFAAʿATU HUM SHAY-ANW WA LAA YUNQI-DHUUN

“Shall I take (other) gods besides Him? If (Allah) Most Gracious should intend some adversity for me of no use whatever will be their intercession for me nor can they deliver me.

26

إِنِّىٓ إِذًۭا لَّفِى ضَلَـٰلٍۢ مُّبِينٍ ۝٢٤

INNII I-DHAL LA-FEE DALAALIM MUBEEN

“I would indeed if I were to do so be in manifest Error.

27

إِنِّىٓ ءَامَنتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ فَٱسْمَعُونِ ۝٢٥

INNII AAMANTU BI-RABBI-KUM FAS-MAʿUUN

“For me I have faith in the Lord of you (all): listen then to me!”

28

قِيلَ ٱدْخُلِ ٱلْجَنَّةَ ۖ قَالَ يَـٰلَيْتَ قَوْمِى يَعْلَمُونَ ۝٢٦

QEELAD-KHULIL-JANNAH, QAALA YAA LAYTA QAWMII YAʿLAMUUN

It was said: “Enter thou the Garden.” He said “Ah me! Would that my People knew (what I know)!

29

بِمَا غَفَرَ لِى رَبِّى وَجَعَلَنِى مِنَ ٱلْمُكْرَمِينَ ۝٢٧

BI MAA GHA-FA-RA LII RAB-BII WA JAʿA-LA-NII MINAL-MUKRAMIIN

“For that my Lord has granted me Forgiveness and has enrolled me among those held in honor!”

30

وَمَآ أَنزَلْنَا عَلَىٰ قَوْمِهِۦ مِنۢ بَعْدِهِۦ مِن جُندٍۢ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا كُنَّا مُنزِلِينَ ۝٢٨

WA MAA ANZALNAA ʿALAA QAWMIHII MIM BAʿDI-HII MIN JUNDIM MINAS-SAMAA-I WA MAA KUN-NAA MUNZILEEN

And We sent not down against his People after Him any hosts from heaven nor was it needful for Us so to do.

31

إِن كَانَتْ إِلَّا صَيْحَةًۭ وَٰحِدَةًۭ فَإِذَا هُمْ خَـٰمِدُونَ ۝٢٩

IN KAANAT ILLAA SAYHATAN WAAHIDATAN FA-I-DHAA HUM KHAAMIDUUN

It was no more than a single mighty Blast and behold! they were (like ashes) quenched and silent.

32

يَـٰحَسْرَةً عَلَى ٱلْعِبَادِ ۚ مَا يَأْتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُوا۟ بِهِۦ يَسْتَهْزِءُونَ ۝٣٠

YAA HASRATAN ʿALAL-ʿIBAAD, MAA YA’TII-HIM MIR-RASUULIN ILLAA KAANUU BI-HII YASTAHZI-UUN

Ah! alas for (My) servants! There comes not an apostle to them but they mock Him!

33

أَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ كَمْ أَهْلَكْنَا قَبْلَهُم مِّنَ ٱلْقُرُونِ أَنَّهُمْ إِلَيْهِمْ لَا يَرْجِعُونَ ۝٣١

A-LAM YARAW-KAM AHLAKNAA QABLA-HUM MINAL-QURUUNI ANNA-HUM ILAY-HIM LAA YARJIʿUUN

See they not how many generations before them We destroyed? Not to them will they return:

34

وَإِن كُلٌّۭ لَّمَّا جَمِيعٌۭ لَّدَيْنَا مُحْضَرُونَ ۝٣٢

WA IN KUL-LUL LAMMAA JAMIIʿUL LADAY-NAA MUHDARUUN

But each one of them all will be brought before Us (for judgment).

35

وَءَايَةٌۭ لَّهُمُ ٱلْأَرْضُ ٱلْمَيْتَةُ أَحْيَيْنَـٰهَا وَأَخْرَجْنَا مِنْهَا حَبًّۭا فَمِنْهُ يَأْكُلُونَ ۝٣٣

WA AAYATUL LAHUMUL-ARDUL-MAYTATU-AH-YAY-NAA-HAA WA AKH-RAJ-NAA MIN-HAA HAB-BAN FA-MIN-HU YA’KULUUN

A Sign for them is the earth that is dead; We do give it life and produce grain therefrom of which you do eat.

36

وَجَعَلْنَا فِيهَا جَنَّـٰتٍۢ مِّن نَّخِيلٍۢ وَأَعْنَـٰبٍۢ وَفَجَّرْنَا فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلْعُيُونِ ۝٣٤

WA JAʿALNAA FII-HAA JANNAATIM MIN NAKHIILIN WA AʿA-NAA-BIN(W) WA FAJ-JARNAA FII-HAA MINAL-ʿUYUUN

And We produce therein orchards with date-palms and Vines and We cause springs to gush forth therein.

37

لِيَأْكُلُوا۟ مِن ثَمَرِهِۦ وَمَا عَمِلَتْهُ أَيْدِيهِمْ ۖ أَفَلَا يَشْكُرُونَ ۝٣٥

LI-YA’KULUU MIN THAMARIHI WA MAA ʿAMILAT-HU AYDII-HIM A-FA-LAA YASH-KURUUN

That they may enjoy the fruits of this (artistry): it was not their hands that made this: will they not then give thanks?

38

سُبْحَـٰنَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلْأَزْوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا مِمَّا تُنۢبِتُ ٱلْأَرْضُ وَمِنْ أَنفُسِهِمْ وَمِمَّا لَا يَعْلَمُونَ ۝٣٦

SUB-HAANAL-LA-DHII KHALAQAL-AZWAAJA KULLA-HAA MIM MAA TUNBITUL-ARDU WA MIN ANFUSI-HIM WA MIM MAA LAA YAʿLAMUUN

Glory to Allah Who created in pairs all things that the earth produces as well as their own (human) kind and (other) things of which they have no knowledge.

39

وَءَايَةٌۭ لَّهُمُ ٱلَّيْلُ نَسْلَخُ مِنْهُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فَإِذَا هُم مُّظْلِمُونَ ۝٣٧

WA-AAYATUL LA-HUMUL-LAYLU NASLA-KHU MIN-HUN-NAHAARA FA-I-DHAA HUM MUZLIMUUN

And a Sign for them is the Night: We withdraw therefrom the Day and behold they are plunged in darkness;

40

وَٱلشَّمْسُ تَجْرِى لِمُسْتَقَرٍّۢ لَّهَا ۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقْدِيرُ ٱلْعَزِيزِ ٱلْعَلِيمِ ۝٣٨

WASH-SHAMSU TAJRII LI-MUSTAQAR-RIL LAHAA DHAALIKA TAQDIIRUL-ʿAZIIZIL-ʿALIIM

And the Sun runs his course for a period determined for him: that is the decree of (Him) the exalted in Might the All-Knowing.

41

وَٱلْقَمَرَ قَدَّرْنَـٰهُ مَنَازِلَ حَتَّىٰ عَادَ كَٱلْعُرْجُونِ ٱلْقَدِيمِ ۝٣٩

WAL-QAMARA QAD-DAR-NAA-HU MANAAZILA HAT-TAA ʿAADA KAL-ʿUR-JUUNIL-QADIIM

And the Moon We have measured for her mansions (to traverse) till she returns like the old (and withered) lower part of date-stalk.

42

لَا ٱلشَّمْسُ يَنۢبَغِى لَهَآ أَن تُدْرِكَ ٱلْقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيْلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِ ۚ وَكُلٌّۭ فِى فَلَكٍۢ يَسْبَحُونَ ۝٤٠

LASH-SHAMSU YANBA-GHII LA-HAA AN TUDRIKAL-QAMARA WA-LAL-LAYLU SAABIQUN-NAHAAR, WA-KULLUN FII FALAKIN YAS-BAHUUN

It is not permitted to the Sun to catch up the Moon nor can the Night outstrip the Day: each (just) swims along in (its own) orbit (according to Law).

43

وَءَايَةٌۭ لَّهُمْ أَنَّا حَمَلْنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُمْ فِى ٱلْفُلْكِ ٱلْمَشْحُونِ ۝٤١

WA-‘AAYATUL LAHUM AN-NAA HAMALNAA DHURRIYATA-HUM FIL-FULKIL-MASH-HUUN

And a Sign for them is that We bore their race (through the flood) in the loaded Ark;

44

وَخَلَقْنَا لَهُم مِّن مِّثْلِهِۦ مَا يَرْكَبُونَ ۝٤٢

WA-KHALAQNAA LAHUM MIM MITH-LIHII MAA YARKABUUN

And We have created for them similar (vessels) on which they ride.

45

وَإِن نَّشَأْ نُغْرِقْهُمْ فَلَا صَرِيخَ لَهُمْ وَلَا هُمْ يُنقَذُونَ ۝٤٣

WA-IN NA-SHA’ NUGH-RIQ-HUM FA-LAA SARIIKHA LAHUM WA-LAA HUM YUNQA-DHUUN

If it were Our Will We could drown them; then would there be no helper (to hear their cry) nor could they be delivered.

46

إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ مِّنَّا وَمَتَـٰعًا إِلَىٰ حِينٍۢ ۝٤٤

IL-LAA RAHMATAM MIN-NAA WA-MATAAʿAN ILAA HEEN

Except by way of Mercy from Us and by way of (worldly) convenience (to serve them) for a time.

47

وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱتَّقُوا۟ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيكُمْ وَمَا خَلْفَكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُرْحَمُونَ ۝٤٥

WA-I-DHAA QEELA LAHUMUT-TAQUU MAA BAYNA AYDII-KUM WA-MAA KHALFA-KUM LAʿAL-LA-KUM TUR-HAMUUN

When they are told “Fear ye that which is before you and that which will be after you in order that ye may receive Mercy” (they turn back).

48

وَمَا تَأْتِيهِم مِّنْ ءَايَةٍۢ مِّنْ ءَايَـٰتِ رَبِّهِمْ إِلَّا كَانُوا۟ عَنْهَا مُعْرِضِينَ ۝٤٦

WA-MAA TA’TII-HIM MIN AAYATIM MIN ‘AAYAATI RABBI-HIM IL-LAA KAANUU ʿAN-HAA MUʿRIDEEN

Not a Sign comes to them from among the Signs of their Lord but they turn away therefrom.

49

وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمْ أَنفِقُوا۟ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ أَنُطْعِمُ مَن لَّوْ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ أَطْعَمَهُۥٓ إِنْ أَنتُمْ إِلَّا فِى ضَلَـٰلٍۢ مُّبِينٍۢ ۝٤٧

WA-I-DHAA QEELA LAHUM ANFIQUU MIM MAA RAZAQA-KUMUL-LAAHU QAA-LAL-LA-DHEENA KAFARUU LIL-LADHEENA AAMANUU A-NUTʿIMU MAL-LAW YA-SHAA-UL-LAAHU ATʿAMA HU, IN ANTUM IL-LAA FEE DALAALIM MUBEEN

And when they are told “Spend ye of (the bounties) with which Allah has provided you.” You Unbelievers say to those who believe: “Shall we then feed those whom if Allah had so willed He would have fed (himself)? Ye are in nothing but manifest error.”

50

وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَـٰذَا ٱلْوَعْدُ إِن كُنتُمْ صَـٰدِقِينَ ۝٤٨

WA-YAQUULUUNA MATAA HAA-DHAL-WAʿDU IN KUNTUM SAADIQEEN

Further they say “When will this promise (come to pass) if what ye say is true?”

51

مَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا صَيْحَةًۭ وَٰحِدَةًۭ تَأْخُذُهُمْ وَهُمْ يَخِصِّمُونَ ۝٤٩

MAA YANZURUUN IL-LAA SAY-HATAN(W) WAAHIDATAN TA’KHU-DHU-HUM WA HUM YA-KHIS-SIMUUN

They will not (have to) wait for aught but a single Blast: it will seize them while they are yet disputing among themselves!

52

فَلَا يَسْتَطِيعُونَ تَوْصِيَةًۭ وَلَآ إِلَىٰٓ أَهْلِهِمْ يَرْجِعُونَ ۝٥٠

FA-LAA YAS-TATIIʿUUNA TAWSIYATAN(W) WA-LAA ILAA AH-LI-HIM YARJIʿUUN

No (chance) will they then have by will to dispose (of their affairs) nor to return to their own people!

53

وَنُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ فَإِذَا هُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَجْدَاثِ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمْ يَنسِلُونَ ۝٥١

WA NUFI-KHA FIS-SUURI FA-I-DHAA HUM MINAL-AJ-DAA-THI ILAA RABBI-HIM YAN-SI-LUUN

The trumpet shall be sounded when behold! from the sepulchers (men) will rush forth to their Lord!

54

قَالُوا۟ يَـٰوَيْلَنَا مَنۢ بَعَثَنَا مِن مَّرْقَدِنَا ۜ ۗ هَـٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنُ وَصَدَقَ ٱلْمُرْسَلُونَ ۝٥٢

QAALUU YAA WAY-LA-NAA MAM BAʿA-THA-NAA MIN MARQADI-NAA, HAA-DHAA MAA WAʿADAR-RAHMAANU WA SADAQAL MURSALUUN

They will say: “Ah! woe unto us! Who hath raised us up from our beds of repose? (A voice will say:)”This is what (Allah) Most Gracious had promised and true was the word of the apostles!”

55

إِن كَانَتْ إِلَّا صَيْحَةًۭ وَٰحِدَةًۭ فَإِذَا هُمْ جَمِيعٌۭ لَّدَيْنَا مُحْضَرُونَ ۝٥٣

IN KAANAT IL-LAA SAY-HATAN(W) WAA-HIDATAN FA-I-DHAA HUM JAMIIʿUL LADAY-NAA MUHDARUUN

It will be no more than a single Blast when lo! they will all be brought up before Us!

56

فَٱلْيَوْمَ لَا تُظْلَمُ نَفْسٌۭ شَيْـًۭٔا وَلَا تُجْزَوْنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ ۝٥٤

FAL-YAWMA LAA TUZ-LAMU NAFSUN SHAY-AN WA-LAA TUJ-ZAW-NA IL-LAA MAA KUNTUM TAʿMALUUN

Then on that Day not a soul will be wronged in the least and ye shall but be repaid the meeds of your past Deeds.

57

إِنَّ أَصْحَـٰبَ ٱلْجَنَّةِ ٱلْيَوْمَ فِى شُغُلٍۢ فَـٰكِهُونَ ۝٥٥

IN-NA AS-HAABAL-JANNATIL-YAW-MA FEE SHU-GHU-LIN FAA-KI-HUUN

Verily the Companions of the Garden shall that Day have joy in all that they do;

58

هُمْ وَأَزْوَٰجُهُمْ فِى ظِلَـٰلٍ عَلَى ٱلْأَرَآئِكِ مُتَّكِـُٔونَ ۝٥٦

HUM WA AZ-WAA-JU-HUM FEE ZILAALIN ʿA-LAL-ARAA-I-KI MUT-TA-KI-UUN

They and their associates will be in groves of (cool) shade reclining on thrones (of dignity);

59

لَهُمْ فِيهَا فَـٰكِهَةٌۭ وَلَهُم مَّا يَدَّعُونَ ۝٥٧

LA-HUM FII-HAA FAA-KI-HA-TAN(W) WA LA-HUM MAA YAD-DAʿUUN

(Every) fruit (enjoyment) will be there for them; they shall have whatever they call for;

60

سَلَـٰمٌۭ قَوْلًۭا مِّن رَّبٍّۢ رَّحِيمٍۢ ۝٥٨

SALAAM, QAW-LAM MIR-RAB-BIR RAHEEM

“Peace! A Word (of salutation) from a Lord Most Merciful!

61

وَٱمْتَـٰزُوا۟ ٱلْيَوْمَ أَيُّهَا ٱلْمُجْرِمُونَ ۝٥٩

WAM-TAA-ZUL-YAW-MA AY-YUHAL-MUJRIMUUN

And O ye in sin! get ye apart this Day!

62

أَلَمْ أَعْهَدْ إِلَيْكُمْ يَـٰبَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ أَن لَّا تَعْبُدُوا۟ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنَ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمْ عَدُوٌّۭ مُّبِينٌۭ ۝٦٠

A-LAM AʿA-HAD I-LAY KUM YAA BANEE AADAMA AL-LAA TAʿBU-DUSH-SHAYTAANA IN-NA-HU LA KUM ʿA-DUU-WUM MUBEEN

“Did I not enjoin on you O ye children of Adam that ye should not worship Satan; for that he was to you an enemy avowed?

63

وَأَنِ ٱعْبُدُونِى ۚ هَـٰذَا صِرَٰطٌۭ مُّسْتَقِيمٌۭ ۝٦١

WA-A-NIʿBU-DUU-NEE, HAA-DHAA SIRAATUM MUSTAQEEM

“And that ye should worship Me (for that) this was the Straight Way?

64

وَلَقَدْ أَضَلَّ مِنكُمْ جِبِلًّۭا كَثِيرًا ۖ أَفَلَمْ تَكُونُوا۟ تَعْقِلُونَ ۝٦٢

WA LA-QAD A-DAL-LA MIN-KUM JIBIL-LAN KA-THEE-RAN A-FA-LAM TAKUUNUU TAʿQI-LUUN

“But he did lead astray a great multitude of you. Did ye not then understand?

65

هَـٰذِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ ٱلَّتِى كُنتُمْ تُوعَدُونَ ۝٦٣

HAA-DHI-HII JAHAN-NAMUL-LATII KUNTUM TUUʿADUUN

“This is the Hell of which ye were (repeatedly) warned!

66

ٱصْلَوْهَا ٱلْيَوْمَ بِمَا كُنتُمْ تَكْفُرُونَ ۝٦٤

IS-LAW-HAL-YAW-MA BI-MAA KUNTUM TAK-FURUUN

“Embrace ye the (Fire) this Day for that ye (persistently) rejected (Truth).”

67

ٱلْيَوْمَ نَخْتِمُ عَلَىٰٓ أَفْوَٰهِهِمْ وَتُكَلِّمُنَآ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَتَشْهَدُ أَرْجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُوا۟ يَكْسِبُونَ ۝٦٥

AL-YAW-MA NAKH-TIMU ʿA-LAA AF-WAAHI-HIM WA TU-KAL-LIMU-NAA AY-DII-HIM WA TASH-HADU ARJULU-HUM BI-MAA KAANUU YAK-SI-BUUN

That Day shall We set a seal on their mouths. But their hands will speak to Us and their feet bear witness to all that they did.

68

وَلَوْ نَشَآءُ لَطَمَسْنَا عَلَىٰٓ أَعْيُنِهِمْ فَٱسْتَبَقُوا۟ ٱلصِّرَٰطَ فَأَنَّىٰ يُبْصِرُونَ ۝٦٦

WA LAW NA-SHAA-U LA TA-MAS-NAA ʿA-LAA AʿA-YUNI-HIM FAS-TA-BA-QUS-SIRAATA FA-AN-NAA YUB-SIRUUN

If it had been Our Will We could surely have blotted out their eyes; then should they have run about groping for the Path but how could they have seen?

69

وَلَوْ نَشَآءُ لَمَسَخْنَـٰهُمْ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِهِمْ فَمَا ٱسْتَطَـٰعُوا۟ مُضِيًّۭا وَلَا يَرْجِعُونَ ۝٦٧

WA LAW NA-SHAA-U LA-MA-SAKH-NAA-HUM ʿA-LAA MA-KAA-NATI-HIM FA-MAS-TA-TAAʿUU MU-DII-YAN(W) WA LAA YAR-JIʿUUN

And if it had been Our Will We could have transformed them (to remain) in their places: then should they have been unable to move about nor could they have returned (after error).

70

وَمَن نُّعَمِّرْهُ نُنَكِّسْهُ فِى ٱلْخَلْقِ ۖ أَفَلَا يَعْقِلُونَ ۝٦٨

WA MAN NUʿAM-MIR-HU NU-NAK-KIS-HU FIL-KHAL-QI A-FA-LAA YAʿQI-LUUN

If We grant long life to any We cause him to be reversed in nature: will they not then understand?

71

وَمَا عَلَّمْنَـٰهُ ٱلشِّعْرَ وَمَا يَنۢبَغِى لَهُۥٓ ۚ إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌۭ وَقُرْءَانٌۭ مُّبِينٌۭ ۝٦٩

WA MAA ʿAL-LAM-NAA-HUSH-SHIʿRA WA MAA YAN-BA-GHII LAHUU IN HUWA IL-LAA DHIK-RUN(W) WA QUR’AANUM MUBIIN

We have not instructed the (Prophet) in Poetry nor is it meet for Him: this is no less than a Message and a Qur’an making things clear:

72

لِّيُنذِرَ مَن كَانَ حَيًّۭا وَيَحِقَّ ٱلْقَوْلُ عَلَى ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ ۝٧٠

LI-YUN-DHIRU MAN KAANA HAY-YAN(W) WA YA-HIQ-QAL-QAWLU ʿA-LAL-KAA-FIRIIN

That it may give admonition to any (who are) alive and that the charge may be proved against those who reject (Truth).

73

أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ أَنَّا خَلَقْنَا لَهُم مِّمَّا عَمِلَتْ أَيْدِينَآ أَنْعَـٰمًۭا فَهُمْ لَهَا مَـٰلِكُونَ ۝٧١

A-WA-LAM YA-RAW AN-NAA KHALAQNAA LA-HUM MIM MAA ʿA-MI-LAT AYDII NAA ANʿAA-MAN FA-HUM LA-HAA MAA-LI-KUUN

See they not that it is We Who have created for them among the things which Our hands have fashioned cattle which are under their dominion?

74

وَذَلَّلْنَـٰهَا لَهُمْ فَمِنْهَا رَكُوبُهُمْ وَمِنْهَا يَأْكُلُونَ ۝٧٢

WA-DHAL-LAL-NAA-HAA LA-HUM FA-MIN-HAA RAKUUBU-HUM WA MIN-HAA YA’KU-LUUN

And that We have subjected them to their (use)? Of them some do carry them and some they eat:

75

وَلَهُمْ فِيهَا مَنَـٰفِعُ وَمَشَارِبُ ۖ أَفَلَا يَشْكُرُونَ ۝٧٣

WA LA-HUM FII-HAA MANAA-FIʿU WA-MA-SHAA-RIBU A-FA-LAA YASH-KU-RUUN

And they have (other) profits from them (besides) and they get (milk) to drink. Will they not then be grateful?

76

وَٱتَّخَذُوا۟ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ ءَالِهَةًۭ لَّعَلَّهُمْ يُنصَرُونَ ۝٧٤

WAT-TA-KHA-DHUU MIN DUUNIL-LAAHI AA-LIHATAL LAʿAL-LA-HUM YUN-SARUUN

Yet they take (for worship) gods other than Allah (hoping) that they might be helped!

77

لَا يَسْتَطِيعُونَ نَصْرَهُمْ وَهُمْ لَهُمْ جُندٌۭ مُّحْضَرُونَ ۝٧٥

LAA YAS-TA-TII-ʿUUNA NAS-RA-HUM WA HUM LA-HUM JUN-DUM MUH-DA-RUUN

They have not the power to help them: but they will be brought up (before Our Judgment-Seat) as a troop (to be condemned).

78

فَلَا يَحْزُنكَ قَوْلُهُمْ ۘ إِنَّا نَعْلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعْلِنُونَ ۝٧٦

FA-LAA YAH-ZUN-KA QAWLU-HUM IN-NAA NAʿLAMU MAA YUSIR-RUUNA WA MAA YUʿLI-NUUN

Let not their speech then grieve thee. Verily We know what they hide as well as what they disclose.

79

أَوَلَمْ يَرَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ أَنَّا خَلَقْنَـٰهُ مِن نُّطْفَةٍۢ فَإِذَا هُوَ خَصِيمٌۭ مُّبِينٌۭ ۝٧٧

A-WA-LAM YA-RAL-IN-SAANA AN-NAA KHALAQ-NAA-HU MIN NUT-FATIN FA-I-DHAA HUWA KHA-SIIMUM MUBIIN

Doth not man see that it is We Who created Him from sperm? Yet behold! He (stands forth) as an open adversary!

80

وَضَرَبَ لَنَا مَثَلًۭا وَنَسِىَ خَلْقَهُۥ ۖ قَالَ مَن يُحْىِ ٱلْعِظَـٰمَ وَهِىَ رَمِيمٌۭ ۝٧٨

WA DA-RA-BA LA-NAA MA-THA-LAN(W) WA NA-SI-YA KHALQAH, QAALA MAN(Y) YUH-YIL-ʿI-ZAAMA WA HIYA RAMIIM

And he makes comparisons for us and forgets his own (Origin and) Creation: He says “Who can give life to (dry) bones and decomposed ones (at that)?”

81

قُلْ يُحْيِيهَا ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنشَأَهَآ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٍۢ ۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ خَلْقٍ عَلِيمٌ ۝٧٩

QUL YUH-YII-HAL-LA-DHII AN-SHA-A-HAA AW-WALA MARRAH, WA-HUWA BI-KUL-LI KHAL-QIN ʿA-LIIM

Say “He will give them life Who created them for the first time! For He is well-versed in every kind of creation!

82

ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّجَرِ ٱلْأَخْضَرِ نَارًۭا فَإِذَآ أَنتُم مِّنْهُ تُوقِدُونَ ۝٨٠

AL-LA-DHII JAʿA-LA LA-KUM MI-NASH-SHA-JARIL-AKH-DARI NAA-RAN FA-I-DHAA ANTUM MIN-HU TUU-QI-DUUN

“The same Who produces for you fire out of the green tree when behold! Ye kindle therewith (your own fires)!

83

أَوَلَيْسَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ بِقَـٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَخْلُقَ مِثْلَهُم ۚ بَلَىٰ وَهُوَ ٱلْخَلَّـٰقُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ ۝٨١

A-WA-LAY-SAL-LA-DHII KHALAQAS-SAMAA-WAATI WAL-ARDA BI-QAA-DIRIN ʿA-LAA AN(Y) YAKH-LU-QU MITH-LA-HUM, BALAA WA-HUWAL-KHAL-LAA-QUL-ʿALIIM

“Is not He Who created the heavens and the earth able to create the like thereof?” Yea indeed! for He is the Creator Supreme of skill and knowledge (infinite)!

84

إِنَّمَآ أَمْرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيْـًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ ۝٨٢

IN-NA-MAA AM-RU-HUU I-DHAA ARAADA SHAY-AN AN(Y) YAQUULA LA-HUU KUN FA YAKUUN

Verily when He intends a thing His command is “Be” and it is!

85

فَسُبْحَـٰنَ ٱلَّذِى بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَىْءٍۢ وَإِلَيْهِ تُرْجَعُونَ ۝٨٣

FA-SUB-HAANAL-LADHII BI-YAD-HII MALAKUUTU KULLI SHAY-IN(W) WA-I-LAY-HI TUR-JAʿUUN

So glory to Him in Whose hands is the dominion of all things; and to Him will ye be all brought back.

Sūratul Fātiḥah
Al-Wirdul ʿĀm
