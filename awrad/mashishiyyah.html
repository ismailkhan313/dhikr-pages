Aṣ-Ṣalātul Mashīshiyyah

Once daily.
الصَّلَاةُ المَشِيشِيَّةُ

Recite once daily. It was composed by Mawlāy ʿAbd as-Salām ibn Mashīsh, the Quṭb of his age and the Shaykh of Imām Abul Ḥasan ash-Shādhilī. Through Imām ash-Shādhilī it passed into the Ṭarīqah, and it stands among the most treasured of its prayers upon the Prophet ﷺ. Its words describe the Prophet ﷺ as the secret from which the lights burst forth, and ask for true knowledge of him and to be drowned in the Ocean of Oneness. It is recited for love and gnosis of the Prophet ﷺ, and for the opening of the heart to the Divine Presence.

1

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيْمِ

A-ʿŪDHU BILLĀHI MINASH SHAYṬĀ-NIR RAJĪM

I seek refuge in Allāh from Shayṭān, the rejected one.

2

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيْمِ

BIS-MIL-LĀHIR RAḤMĀ-NIR RAḤĪM

In the Name of Allah, The Universally Merciful, The Singularly Compassionate.

3

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِىِّ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسْلِيمًا﴾

IN-NALLĀHA WA MALĀ-IKATAHU YUṢALLŪNA ʿALAN-NABIY,
YĀ AY-YUHAL-LADHĪNA ĀMANŪ ṢALLŪ ʿALAYHI WA SAL-LIMŪ TASLĪMĀ

Verily, Allāh and His Angels send blessings on the Messenger of Allāh. O you who believe, invoke blessings on him and salute him with a worthy salutation.

4

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مَنْ مِنْهُ انْشَقَّتِ الْأَسْرَارُ

ALLĀHUMMA ṢALLI ʿALĀ MAN MINHUN SHAQ-QATIL ASRĀR

O Allah shower Your blessings upon him from whom burst open the secrets,

5

وَانْفَلَقَتِ الْأَنْوَارُ

WAN FALAQATIL ANWĀR

from whom stream forth the lights,

6

وَفِيهِ ارْتَقَتِ الْحقَائِقُ

WA FĪHIR TAQATIL ḤAQĀ’IQ

and in whom rise up the realities,

7

وَتَنَزَّلَتْ عُلُومُ آدَمَ فَأَعْجَزَ الْخَلَائِقِ

WA TANAZ-ZALAT ʿULŪMU ĀDAMA FA AʿJAZAL KHALĀ’IQ

and upon whom descended the sciences of Adam, by which all creatures are made powerless

8

وَلَهُ تَضَاءَلَتِ الْفُهُومُ

WA LAHU TADĀ ’ALATIL FUHŪM

and blessings upon him before whom all understanding is diminished.

9

فَلَمْ يُدْرِكْهُ مِنَّا سَابِقٌ وَلَا لَاحِقٌ

FA LAM YUDRIK-HU MIN-NA SĀBIQUN WA LĀ LĀḤIQ

None of us totally comprehend him, whether in the past or the future.

10

فَرِيَاضُ الْمَلَكُوتِ بِزَهْرِ جَمَالِهِ مُونِقَةٌ

FA RIYĀḌUL MALAKŪTI BIZAHRI JAMĀLIHI MŪNIQAH

The gardens of the spiritual kingdom blossom ornately with the resplendence of his beauty,

11

وَحِيَاضُ الْجَبَرُوتِ بِفَيْضِ أَنْوَارِهِ مُتَدَفِّقَةٌ

WA ḤIYĀDUL JABARŪTI BIFAYḌI ANWĀRIHI MUTADAF-FIQAH

and the reservoirs of the World of Dominion overflow with the outpouring of his light.

12

وَلَا شَيْءَ إِلَّا وَهُوَ بِهِ مَنُوطٌ

WA LĀ SHAY’A ILLĀ WA HUWA BIHI MANŪṬ

There is nothing that is not connected to him,

13

إِذْ لَوْلَا الْوَاسِطَةُ لَذَهَبَ كَمَا قِيلَ الْمَوْسُوطُ

IDH LAWLAL WĀSIṬATU LA-DHAHABA KAMĀ QĪLAL MAWSŪṬ

because if there were no intercessor, everything to be interceded for would vanish, as it is said.

14

صَلَاةً تَلِيقُ بِكَ مِنْكَ إِلَيْهِ كَمَا هُوَ أَهْلُهُ

ṢALĀTAN TALĪQU BIKA MINKA ILAYHI KAMĀ HUWA AHLUH

So bless him with a prayer that is worthy of You, from You, as befits his stature.

15

اَللَّهُمَّ إِنَّهُ سِرُّكَ الْجَامِعُ الدَّالُّ عَلَيْكَ

ALLĀHUMMA IN-NAHU SIRRUKAL JĀMIʿUD-DĀLU ʿALAYK

O Allah indeed he is Your all-encompassing secret that leads through You to You,

16

وَحِجَابُكَ الْأَعْظَمُ الْقَائِمُ لَكَ بَيْنَ يَدَيْكَ

WA ḤIJĀBUKAL AʿẒAMUL QĀ’IMU LAKA BAYNA YADAYK

and he is Your Supreme Veil raised before You, between Your Hands.

17

اَللَّهُمَّ أَلْحِقْنِي بِنَسَبِهِ

ALLĀHUMMA AL-ḤIQNI BI-NASABIH

O Allah include me among his descendants,

18

وَحَقِّقْنِي بِحَسَبِهِ

WA ḤAQ-QIQNĪ BI ḤASABIH

and confirm me through his account,

19

وَعَرِّفْنِي إِيَّاهُ مَعْرِفَةً أَسْلَمُ بِهَا مِنْ مَوَارِدِ الْجَهْلِ

WA ʿAR-RIFNĪ IYYĀHU MAʿRIFATAN ASLAMU BIHĀ MIN MAWĀRIDIL JAHL

and let me know him with a deep knowledge that keeps me safe from the wells of ignorance,

20

وَأَكْرَعُ بِهَا مِنْ مَوَارِدِ الْفَضْلِ

WA AKRAʿU BIHĀ MIN MAWĀRIDIL FAḌL

so that I might drink to fullness from the wells of excellence.

21

وَاحْمِلْنِي عَلىٰ سَبِيلِهِ إِلَىٰ حَضْرَتِكَ

WAḤMILNĪ ʿALĀ SABĪLIHI ILĀ ḤADRATIK

Carry me on his path to Your Presence

22

حَمْلًا مَحْفُوفًا بِنُصْرَتِكَ

ḤAMLAN MAḤFŪFAN BI-NUṢRATIK

encompassed by Your Victory,

23

وَاقْذِفْ بِي عَلَى الْبَاطِلِ فَأَدْمَغَهُ

WAQDHIF BĪ ʿALAL BĀṬILI FA-ADMAGHAH

and strike through me at the false so that I may destroy it.

24

وَزُجَّ بِي فِي بِحَارِ الْأَحَدِيَّةِ

WA ZUJ-JA BĪ FĪ BIḤĀRIL AḤADIY-YAH

Plunge me into the seas of Oneness,

25

وَانْشُلْنِي مِنْ أَوْحَالِ التَّوْحِيدِ

WANSHULNĪ MIN AWḤĀLIT-TAWḤĪD

pull me out of the morass of metaphorical Unity,

26

وَأَغْرِقْنِي فِي عَيْنِ بَحْرِ الْوَحْدَةِ

WA AGHRIQNĪ FĪ ʿAYNI BAḤRIL WAḤDAH

and drown me in the Essence of the Ocean of Unicity

27

حَتَّىٰ لَا أَرَىٰ وَلَا أَسْمَعَ وَلَا أَجِدَ وَلَا أُحِسَّ إِلَّا بِهَا

ḤATTĀ LĀ ARĀ WA LĀ ASMAʿA WA LĀ AJIDA WA LĀ UḤISSA ILLĀ BIHĀ

until I neither see, nor hear, nor find, nor sense, except through It.

28

وَاجْعَلِ الْحِجَابَ الْأَعْظَمَ حَيَاةَ رُوحِي

WAJʿALIL ḤIJĀBAL AʿA-ẒAMA ḤAYĀTA RŪḤI

O Allah make the Supreme Veil the life of my spirit

29

وَرُوحَهُ سِرَّ حَقِيقَتِي

WA RŪḤAHU SIRRA ḤAQĪQATĪ

and make his spirit the secret of my reality,

30

وَحَقِيقَتَهُ جَامِعَ عَوَالِمِي

WA ḤAQĪQATAHU JĀMIʿA ʿAWĀLIMĪ

and his soul the secret of my reality

31

بِتَحْقِيقِ الْحَقِّ الْأَوَّلِ

BITAḤQĪQIL ḤAQ-QIL AWWAL

and his reality the conflux of my worlds through the realization of the First Truth

32

يَا أَوَّلُ يَا آخِر

YĀ AWWALU YĀ ĀKHIR

O First! O Last!

33

يَا ظَاهِرُ يَا بَاطِن

YĀ ẒĀHIRU YĀ BĀṬIN

O Manifest! O Most Hidden!

34

اِسْمَعْ نِدآئِي بِمَا سَمِعْتَ بِهِ نِدَآءَ عَبْدِكَ زَكَرِيَّا

ISMʿA NIDĀ’I BIMĀ SAMIʿTA BIHI NIDĀ’A ʿABDIKA ZAKARIYYĀ

Hear my call as You heard the call of your servant Zakariya

35

وَانْصُرْنِي بِكَ لَكَ

WANṢURNI BIKA LAK

and grant me victory through You, for You

36

وَأَيِّدْنِي بِكَ لَكَ

WA AY-YIDNI BIKA LAK

and support me through You, for You

37

وَاجْمَعْ بَيْنِي وَبَيْنَكَ

WAJMAʿ BAYNĪ WA BAYNAK

and join me to You

38

وَحُلْ بَيْنِي وَبَيْنَ غَيْرِكَ

WA ḤUL BAYNĪ WA BAYNA GHAYRIK

and come between myself and anything other than You—

39

الله الله الله

ALLĀĀĀH ALLĀĀĀH ALLĀĀĀH

Allaaah! Allaaah! Allaaah!
[Hold each ĀĀĀ for twelve slow measures]

40

إِنَّ الَّذِي فَرَضَ عَلَيْكَ الْقُرْآنَ لَرَادُّكَ إِلَىٰ مَعَادٍ

IN-NALLADHĪ FARAḌA ʿALAYKAL QUR’ĀNA LA-RĀD-DUKA ILĀ MAʿĀD

Indeed He, Who ordained the Qur’an for you, will return you to the station of your Ultimate Destiny.

41×3

﴿رَبَّنَا آتِنَا مِنْ لَّدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا﴾

RABBANĀ ĀTINĀ MIN LADUNKA RAḤMATAN WA HAYYI’ LANĀ MIN AMRINĀ RASHADA

﴾O Lord grant us from Your Presence Mercy and endow us, whatever our outward condition, with sure Guidance!﴿

42

﴿إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ وَمَلَـٰٓئِكَتَهُۥ يُصَلُّونَ عَلَى ٱلنَّبِىِّ يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ صَلُّواْ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلِّمُواْ تَسْلِيمًا﴾

IN-NALLĀHA WA MALĀ-IKATAHU YUṢALLŪNA ʿALAN-NABIY, YĀ AY-YUHAL-LADHĪNA ĀMANŪ ṢALLŪ ʿALAYHI WA SAL-LIMŪ TASLĪMĀ

﴾Verily, Allāh and His Angels send blessings on the Messenger of Allāh. O you who believe, invoke blessings on him and salute him with a worthy salutation.﴿

43

صَلَوَاتُ اللهِ وَسَلَامُهُ وَتَحِيَّاتُهُ وَرَحَمَاتُهُ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ

ṢALAWĀTULLĀHI WA SALĀMUHU WA TAḤIYYĀTUHU WA RAḤAMĀTUHU WA BARAKĀTUHU ʿALĀ SAYYIDINĀ MUḤAMMAD

44

عَبْدِكَ وَنَبِيِّكَ وَرَسُولِكَ النَّبِيِّ الأُمِّيِّ وَعَلَى آلِهِ وَصَحْبِهِ وَسَلِّمْ

ʿABDIKA WA NABIYYIKA WA RASŪLIKAN-NABI-YIL-UMMIYYI WA ʿALĀ ĀLIHI WA ṢAḤBIHI WA SALLIM

45

عَدَدَ الشَّفْعِ وَالْوَتْرِ وَعَدَدَ كَلِمَاتِ رَبِّنَا التَّامَّاتِ الْمُبَارَكَاتِ

ʿADADASH-SHAFʿI WAL-WATRI WA ʿADADA KALIMĀTI RABBINAT-TĀMĀTIL MUBĀRAKĀT

46

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ وَسَلَامٌ عَلَى الْمُرْسَلِينَ

SUBḤĀNA RABBIKA RABBIL ʿIZZATI ʿAMA YAṢIFŪN WA SALĀMUN ʿALAL-MURSALĪN

47

وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

WAL-ḤAMDU LILLĀHI RABBIL ʿĀLAMĪN

48

Make Duʿāʾ for your Shaykh, his family, for yourself, your parents, your fellow Murids on this Path, and for all the Muslims. (Refer to Duʿāʾ after Dhikr to proceed.)

Al-Laṭīfiyyah
Al-Musabbaʿāt al-ʿAshr
